## IEEE Registration Authority data

`data/vendors.tsv.gz` is a compact snapshot of public MA-L, MA-M and MA-S registration records downloaded on 2026-10-23 from:

- https://standards-oui.ieee.org/oui/oui.csv
- https://standards-oui.ieee.org/oui28/mam.csv
- https://standards-oui.ieee.org/oui36/oui36.csv

The snapshot contains prefix, organization name and registry type. It does not contain the original postal-address column. It is not a record of any user's device or local network.

IEEE registration information and organization names remain attributable to their respective owners. The project's MIT license covers original application code, not the registry data. See the [IEEE Registration Authority](https://standards.ieee.org/products-programs/regauth/) for the authoritative data and applicable terms.
